Sunk Cost Fallacy

Finishing something bad because you already started it.

What it means

Sticking with a choice just because you've already invested time, money, or effort. The investment is gone either way (this is called the sunk cost fallacy).

Analogy

Finishing a terrible movie because you paid for the ticket. The money is gone either way — but now you're also losing your time.

Reflect on this

Name one project, subscription, or commitment you're keeping alive mainly because of what you've already put into it. What would you do if you discovered it today for the first time?
